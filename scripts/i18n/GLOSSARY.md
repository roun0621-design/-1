Rules
- Keep placeholders exactly: `{0}`, `{1}` … (they are substituted at runtime). Keep leading/trailing punctuation like "…", ":", "(", ")".
- Do not translate product names: PACE RISE, Node, P-R : Node, FinishLynx, Excel, PDF, Aligo(알리고), WaveLight.
- Keep unit tokens: m, cm, kg, m/s, 초(seconds)→"s" when it is a unit suffix.
- Buttons/labels: short imperative English ("Save", "Generate start list"). Sentences: plain, no exclamation marks.
- Korean honorific/notice phrasing ("~합니다", "~하세요") → neutral English ("Saved.", "Select a competition.").
- Korean counters: "{0}개" → "{0}", "{0}명" → "{0} athletes" (or "{0}" if it is a pure count next to a noun), "{0}건" → "{0} items".
- Do not change the meaning; when unsure keep it literal and short.
- JA: use standard Japanese athletics (JAAF) terms; polite plain form (です/ます) for sentences, noun phrases for labels.
